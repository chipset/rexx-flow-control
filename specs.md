# REXX Control Flow Specifications

This project’s specification is split into two documents:

1. **Product Specification**
   - File: `product-spec.md`
   - Focus: product goals, users, features, workflows, constraints, and release-level behavior

2. **Technical Design Specification**
   - File: `technical-design-spec.md`
   - Focus: architecture, parser/analysis model, session model, trust boundaries, persistence, exports, and verification

## Recommended reading order
1. `product-spec.md`
2. `technical-design-spec.md`
